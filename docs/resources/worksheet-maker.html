Make your own mad minute worksheet

Choose the operation and the range of numbers, then print. Every click makes a fresh sheet. Nothing you choose is sent anywhere; it all happens in your browser.

Subtraction never gives a negative answer and division always comes out even, like the app. For division, the numbers are the facts you want to practise: 1 to 12 gives everything up to 12 x 12.

Practise it on a phone or tablet

The free Math Mad Minute app runs the same drill with a timer (30, 60 or 120 seconds), shows every answer afterwards, and keeps track of the facts you miss. No ads, no account, and everything stays on the device.
