How to use a mad minute

Start with a range the child can mostly answer, and have them beat their own score rather than anyone else's. Practise a little every day. When a few facts keep going wrong, drill those on their own instead of repeating the whole sheet.

Practise it on a phone or tablet

The free Math Mad Minute app runs the same drill with a timer (30, 60 or 120 seconds), shows every answer afterwards, and keeps track of the facts you miss. No ads, no account, and everything stays on the device.
