# Math Mad Minute Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026

Math Mad Minute does not collect or share any personal or non-personal data about its users.

## What stays on your device

To show your progress, the app saves your settings and a short history of your finished rounds (scores, the operations and round length you used, and when you played) on your own device. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

You can delete it at any time with "Clear all history" in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

## Third-party services

The app does not use third-party services that collect data, such as analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for all users, including children. Because it collects no data, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. The full history of changes is public in the [repository](https://github.com/bsav0016/MadMinute/commits/master/docs/privacy.md).

## Contact us

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at <https://github.com/bsav0016/MadMinute/issues>.
