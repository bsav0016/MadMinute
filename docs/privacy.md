# Math Mad Minute Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026

Math Mad Minute does not collect or share any personal information about its users. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

To show your progress, the app saves your settings, a short history of your finished rounds (scores, the operations and round length you used, and when you played), and a small record of each problem you have answered (how often you got it right and how long correct answers took) on your own device. The app uses the per-problem record to show your trouble spots and times table, and, if you turn it on, to ask more of the problems you find hard. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

The app can keep separate progress for up to 12 players on one device. A player is named by the app from a fixed list of words and animals (for example "Swift Fox"); nobody types a name, so no personal details are entered. Each player's settings, history and problem record stay on the device.

The app also keeps each player's daily goal, their streak (the days in a row they played and the best streak) and the days and number of rounds played today, so it can show them on the home screen and in the table levels. This too stays on the device. A reminder, if you turn it on, is a time of day saved on the device.

You can delete all of it at any time with "Clear all history" in the app (which also starts the streak again), or by uninstalling the app.

## Sharing and backups

Two buttons create text that you can send somewhere: "Share Score" on the results screen (your score, the setup and a link to the app) and "Back up progress" in the players sheet (a player's progress, including their goal and streak). The text is made only when you tap the button, and it goes only where you choose in your phone's own share window. Nothing is sent to us. To restore a backup you paste the text into the app, which adds it as a new player on that device.

## App updates

To fix problems without waiting for an app store review, the app asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) each time it opens whether a newer version of the app's code is available. That request carries what any internet request carries, such as your IP address, plus the app version and your device's platform, and the service uses it to send the right update. Your settings, history and players are not part of it, and we do not use it to identify anyone. Expo's own policy applies to how it handles those requests: <https://expo.dev/privacy>.

## Daily reminder

The app can send you a reminder once a day. It is off until you turn it on, and the phone asks for your permission only at that moment. The reminder is a notification that the app asks your phone to show at the time you choose, so it is created and shown on the device: nothing about it is sent to us or to anyone else, the app does not use push notifications from a server, and the text ("Ready for a minute of math practice?") does not name or describe the player. You can turn it off in the app at any time, or in your phone's notification settings. On Android the app may also ask the phone to start the reminders again after the phone restarts.

## Ratings

After a new personal best the app may ask you to rate it using the App Store's or Google Play's own rating window. The store runs that window; the app does not receive your answer.

## Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising or crash reporting. The only outside service it contacts is the update service described above. Reminders use your phone's own notification system and contact no service.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for all users, including children. It does not ask for or collect personal information from children under 13, and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The only information that leaves the device without a tap from the player is the update request described above, which contains nothing about the child. The streak, goal and reminder are on the device only, use no names, and the app has no points, rankings or messages to other people.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. The full history of changes is public in the [repository](https://github.com/bsav0016/MadMinute/commits/master/docs/privacy.md).
