# Math Mad Minute Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026

Math Mad Minute does not collect or share any personal information about its users. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

To show your progress, the app saves your settings, a short history of your finished rounds (scores, the operations and round length you used, and when you played), and a small record of each problem you have answered (how often you got it right and how long correct answers took) on your own device. The app uses the per-problem record to show your trouble spots and times table, and, if you turn it on, to ask more of the problems you find hard. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

The app can keep separate progress for up to 12 players on one device. A player is named by the app from a fixed list of words and animals (for example "Swift Fox"); nobody types a name, so no personal details are entered. Each player's settings, history and problem record stay on the device.

You can delete all of it at any time with "Clear all history" in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

## Sharing and backups

Two buttons create text that you can send somewhere: "Share Score" on the results screen (your score, the setup and a link to the app) and "Back up progress" in the players sheet (a player's progress). The text is made only when you tap the button, and it goes only where you choose in your phone's own share window. Nothing is sent to us. To restore a backup you paste the text into the app, which adds it as a new player on that device.

## App updates

To fix problems without waiting for an app store review, the app asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) each time it opens whether a newer version of the app's code is available. That request carries what any internet request carries, such as your IP address, plus the app version and your device's platform, and the service uses it to send the right update. Your settings, history and players are not part of it, and we do not use it to identify anyone. Expo's own policy applies to how it handles those requests: <https://expo.dev/privacy>.

## Ratings

After a new personal best the app may ask you to rate it using the App Store's or Google Play's own rating window. The store runs that window; the app does not receive your answer.

## Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising or crash reporting. The only outside service it contacts is the update service described above.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for all users, including children. It does not ask for or collect personal information from children under 13, and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The only information that leaves the device without a tap from the player is the update request described above, which contains nothing about the child.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. The full history of changes is public in the [repository](https://github.com/bsav0016/MadMinute/commits/master/docs/privacy.md).
