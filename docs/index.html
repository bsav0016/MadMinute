Math Mad Minute

The classic timed arithmetic drill, on your phone. Answer as many problems as you can in 30, 60 or 120 seconds, choose which operations and number ranges to practise, then review every answer. It keeps your personal bests, your accuracy for each operation and a times table coloured by the facts you know. No ads, no account, and your history stays on your device.

The free Math Mad Minute app runs the same drill with a timer (30, 60 or 120 seconds), shows every answer afterwards, and keeps track of the facts you miss. No ads, no account, and everything stays on the device.
